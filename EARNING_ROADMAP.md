## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

```
Expected CAC: $5-20 per customer

Example:
If you spend $100 on Google Ads
And get 5 customers
CAC = $100 / 5 = $20

Pay-back period:
If Starter customer pays $4.99/month:
Payback: $20 / $4.99 = 4 months
If customer stays 12+ months: Good ROI! ✓
```

## Success Metrics

**Track These:**
```
Month 1: First paying customer
Month 2: 5 paying customers
Month 3: 20 paying customers
Month 6: 50+ paying customers
MRR Target: $1,000/month
```

**Formulas:**
```
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):
= (Starter subscribers × $4.99) + (Pro subscribers × $14.99)

Churn Rate:
= (Customers lost / Starting customers) × 100
Target: <5% per month

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):
= Average subscription length × Average monthly payment
Example: 12 months × $10 = $120 CLV
```
